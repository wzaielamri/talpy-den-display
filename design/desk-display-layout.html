Desk Display Layout

The 800 × 480 CrowPanel screen for your clock, weather and Claude jobs, drawn with LVGL's own Montserrat sizes so text takes the room it will take on the board. Build the normal screen in step 5; the alert is for step 9.

Hover a row in the table below to find its box on the screen. Example data: 2 jobs working, 1 waiting, 1 done.

Fonts

Switch these on in include/lv_conf.h. A label is as tall as its font's line height.

FontLine heightUsed for
montserrat_1416 pxcaptions, section titles (on by default)
montserrat_2427 pxdate, job names, weather text
montserrat_4044 pxjob counts, alert job name
montserrat_4852 pxclock, temperature

These fonts hold ASCII, °, • and the LV_SYMBOL_… icons. Other characters, such as · or arrows, show as nothing.

Build it in this order

  1. Make one card style with lv_style_t: background, radius 16, padding 20, border 0. Add it to each card and remove LV_OBJ_FLAG_SCROLLABLE.
  2. Three empty cards in their places. Check the gaps on the board before anything else.
  3. Header: clock, date, the PC pill.
  4. Weather card, the sun and cloud last (later objects draw on top).
  5. Brightness card with your slider and its curve.
  6. Jobs card: one row first, then make a function that builds a row and call it four times.
  7. Step 9: the alert goes on lv_layer_top(), so it covers the whole screen.

Reading the numbers

  • Place is the alignment and offset for lv_obj_align(obj, ALIGN, x, y). TOP_LEFT counts from the top-left corner.
  • Inside a card, offsets count from the card's inner edge, after its padding. A card at x 16 with padding 20 starts its children at x 36 on the screen.
  • auto size means LV_SIZE_CONTENT: the label is as wide as its text.
  • Rows 2 to 4 of the job list are row 1 moved down 48 px each.
  • On the screen, y grows downward: y 0 is the top edge.
