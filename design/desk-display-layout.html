Desk Display Layout

The 800 × 480 CrowPanel screen for your clock, weather and Claude jobs, drawn with LVGL's own Montserrat sizes so text takes the room it will take on the board. Build the normal screen in step 5. Tapping the weather card opens the hourly forecast, which gets real data in step 8; a swipe down from the top brings the brightness. When nobody looks (2 min without a touch, or the PC away), a dimmed clock face takes the screen. The alert is for step 9.

Build it in this order

  1. Make one card style with lv_style_t: background, radius 16, padding 20, border 0. Add it to each card and remove LV_OBJ_FLAG_SCROLLABLE.
  2. Three empty cards in their places. Check the gaps on the board before anything else.
  3. Header: clock, date, the PC pill.
  4. Weather card: the labels, then weather_icon_create last (later objects draw on top).
  5. Usage card: the title and the two column heads, then one account's line (name, two bars, two %), made twice.
  6. Jobs card: the banner first (headline, counts, “updated”, then Talpy last, so he draws on top), then one row, then a function that builds a row, called four times.
  7. Pull-down panel on lv_layer_top(): the zone over the header, then the scrim, then the panel with your brightness slider in it.
  8. Hourly forecast: one more card over the three, hidden until the weather card is tapped. Columns first, the temperature line last.
  9. Step 9: the alert is a screen of its own, with Talpy peeking over its box.

Reading the numbers

  • Place is the alignment and offset for lv_obj_align(obj, ALIGN, x, y). TOP_LEFT counts from the top-left corner.
  • Inside a card, offsets count from the card's inner edge, after its padding. A card at x 16 with padding 20 starts its children at x 36 on the screen.
  • auto size means LV_SIZE_CONTENT: the label is as wide as its text.
  • Rows 2 to 4 of the job list are row 1 moved down 48 px each.
  • Hourly columns 2 to 8 are column 1 moved right 91 px each. The temperature line's 8 points sit on the column centres.
  • Inside the jobs banner, offsets count from inside its 2 px border: the headline's x 16 is 18 px from the banner's edge.
  • On the screen, y grows downward: y 0 is the top edge.
